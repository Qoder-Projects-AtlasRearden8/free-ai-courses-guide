The honest math

Free is a spectrum. Here’s the map.

“Free” can mean a full course, a preview, a free lesson inside a paid program, or a certificate that costs money after you finish. That is exactly the kind of fine print this guide makes big enough to read.

Read the certificate map →
01

Free to learn

Lessons, notebooks, books, and videos you can open without paying. The provider may still ask you to create an account.

02

Free certificate

A completion record issued by the provider. Useful, real, and not an accredited degree or a substitute for supervised practice.

03

Paid credential

Professional certificates and verified exams are separate purchases. Worth considering later—not a prerequisite for your first lesson.

A humane on-ramp

A 90-day plan for a real life.

Three short sessions a week. Two flex days in every month. Clear finish lines, a fallback for the weeks life catches fire, and 37 small tasks—not a productivity contest.

Open the 90-day plan →
Days 1–30

See clearly

Get the vocabulary, practice a real prompt, and build a habit of checking the output. The goal is calm competence, not speed.

Days 31–60

Build the mechanics

Add Python and data skills, train a first model, and practice honest evaluation. Keep the artifacts small enough to finish.

Days 61–90

Make it real

Connect an LLM to code, understand agent loops and MCP, then ship one small, documented tool with safety boundaries.
