The honest math

Free is a spectrum. Here’s the map.

“Free” can mean a full course, a preview, a free lesson inside a paid program, or a certificate that costs money after you finish. That is exactly the kind of fine print this guide makes big enough to read.

Made for real people

AI, explained like a friend would.

This shelf is a project of ASDALE DYNAMICS, LLC, founded by Dylan Asdale—a registered nurse turned AI author. It follows the same idea as The Massive AI Manual and his hub at dylanasdale.github.io: plain English, step by step, never condescending.

The guide is designed for people changing fields, running small businesses, writing books, or simply trying to keep up without getting sold a fantasy. Healthcare examples are for education only—never medical advice.

How this guide was made →
  • Official doors only. Links go to the provider’s own site—not a course mill or a reseller.
  • Private by default. No analytics, fonts, ads, or accounts. Progress is stored locally.
  • Honest about money. Free lessons, free certificates, and paid credentials are labeled differently.
  • Built for low-energy days. The smallest useful version of a task is always welcome.
