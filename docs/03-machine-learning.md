# 03. Machine learning, with honest evaluation

**Beginner to intermediate · 3 hours to a full semester**

> Go from your first decision tree to the project-level judgment that separates a useful result from a misleading score.

[Guide home](README.md) · [Certificate guide](certificates.md) · [Learning paths](learning-paths.md) · [Full library](full-library.md)

Machine learning is where data, statistics, and code meet. The point is not to collect algorithms; it is to ask a clear question, establish a baseline, measure honestly, and state what your result does not prove.

Kaggle's Intro to Machine Learning is the kindest first step: three hours, a decision tree, a random forest, and the beginnings of validation. Google's Machine Learning Crash Course adds the conceptual layer with videos and exercises, while Kaggle's Intermediate course introduces pipelines, missing values, and cross-validation.

Only then decide whether you want a larger commitment. Coursera's Machine Learning Specialization is a respected foundation, but the current free preview covers the first module and does not include graded labs or a certificate. edX's CS50 AI is free to audit and much heavier; its verified certificate is paid. Neither is a prerequisite for building something small.

Use books and video selectively. Machine Learning Yearning is short and unusually practical about project decisions. An Introduction to Statistical Learning is a patient reference, not a cover-to-cover requirement. StatQuest and Kaggle's channel are useful when a specific algorithm refuses to click.

**Best for:** Learners with basic Python who want to understand data, models, and evaluation—and builders who need to know when a result is too fragile to trust.

## Courses, one by one

### [Kaggle: Intro to Machine Learning](https://www.kaggle.com/learn/intro-to-machine-learning)

**Kaggle Learn** · Beginner · 3 hours · Interactive notebooks

A three-hour first pass at machine learning with scikit-learn. Build a decision tree and random forest, use a train/test split, and learn why validation matters. No prior ML needed.

- **Access:** Free
- **Certificate:** Free Kaggle Learn completion certificate.
- **Topics:** Machine learning, scikit-learn, Python

### [Machine Learning Crash Course](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course)

**Google** · Beginner · ~15 hours · Interactive course

Google's interactive tour of machine learning fundamentals: regression, classification, neural networks, large language models, and fairness. Videos, readings, and hands-on exercises make it a strong free foundation.

- **Access:** Free
- **Certificate:** No certificate is listed; it is an open Google learning resource.
- **Topics:** Machine learning, Neural networks, Responsible AI

### [Kaggle: Intermediate Machine Learning](https://www.kaggle.com/learn/intermediate-machine-learning)

**Kaggle Learn** · Intermediate · 4 hours · Interactive notebooks

Move beyond a first model with missing-value handling, categorical variables, pipelines, cross-validation, and gradient boosting. Good next practice after Intro to Machine Learning.

- **Access:** Free
- **Certificate:** Free Kaggle Learn completion certificate.
- **Topics:** Machine learning, Pipelines, XGBoost

### [CS50's Introduction to Artificial Intelligence with Python](https://www.edx.org/learn/artificial-intelligence/harvard-university-cs50-s-introduction-to-artificial-intelligence-with-python)

**edX** · Intermediate · 7 weeks, 10–30 hrs/week · edX course

Harvard's seven-week course on search, knowledge, uncertainty, optimization, learning, neural networks, and language, with substantial Python projects. Audit for free; a verified certificate currently costs US$299.

- **Access:** Free audit
- **Certificate:** Free audit track, no certificate. A verified CS50 AI certificate currently costs US$299.
- **Topics:** AI, Python, Computer science

### [Machine Learning Specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/machine-learning-introduction)

**DeepLearning.AI** · Intermediate · ~54 hours · Coursera specialization

Andrew Ng's three-course foundation in supervised learning, advanced learning algorithms, and unsupervised learning or recommender systems. Preview the first module free; graded labs and the certificate require payment or financial aid.

- **Access:** Free first module
- **Certificate:** The Coursera preview is free but does not include graded labs or a certificate. Paid enrollment or approved financial aid unlocks the full specialization.
- **Topics:** Machine learning, Python, Mathematics

### [Machine Learning with Python](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/machine-learning-with-python)

**freeCodeCamp** · Intermediate · ~300 hours (self-paced) · Interactive curriculum + projects

A long-form, project-based TensorFlow and Keras certification covering neural networks, classification, clustering, and recommender systems. Five projects lead to a free certificate; it is a marathon, not a weekend.

- **Access:** Free
- **Certificate:** Free freeCodeCamp certification after completing the required projects.
- **Topics:** Machine learning, TensorFlow, Projects


## Free books and reference

- [Machine Learning Yearning](https://info.deeplearning.ai/machine-learning-yearning-book) — DeepLearning.AI. Beginner, ~3 hours. Andrew Ng's free ebook on shaping machine-learning projects: choosing metrics, prioritizing errors, and deciding what to improve next. Short chapters, little math, and unusually practical for beginners and managers.
- [An Introduction to Statistical Learning](https://www.statlearning.com/) — StatLearning. Intermediate, Long reference. A respected, approachable textbook on statistical learning, with free PDFs and companion labs in R and Python. It is a patient bridge from classical statistics to modern machine learning.

## Official channels

- [StatQuest with Josh Starmer](https://www.youtube.com/@statquest) — StatQuest. All levels, Watch one playlist. Clear, cheerful explanations of statistics and machine-learning algorithms, from histograms to random forests and boosting. The songs are optional; the step-by-step diagrams are the reason to stay.
- [Kaggle](https://www.youtube.com/@Kaggle) — Kaggle. Intermediate, Ongoing. Competition walkthroughs, agent course sessions, and practical data-science talks from Kaggle's community. Good for seeing how other people approach messy, real datasets.

## Certificate reality

Kaggle's Intro and Intermediate Machine Learning certificates are free, as are freeCodeCamp's project certifications after their much longer requirements. Coursera and edX offer free learning access but charge for certificates. Books and YouTube channels do not include certificates.

A completion certificate is a record that you finished a defined piece of learning. It is not an accredited degree, a professional license, or proof that you can do the work unsupervised. See [the certificate guide](certificates.md) for the provider-by-provider rules.

## A small proof of work

Choose one public dataset and one clear question. Write down a baseline before modeling, train a simple model, report two appropriate metrics, and finish with a README section titled "What this does not prove."
