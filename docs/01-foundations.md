# 01. AI foundations, without the jargon

**Beginner · 45 minutes to ~30 hours**

> Understand what AI and generative AI actually are, where they fail, and how to use them without surrendering your judgment.

[Guide home](README.md) · [Certificate guide](certificates.md) · [Learning paths](learning-paths.md) · [Full library](full-library.md)

Start here if you want the vocabulary and a sound mental model before touching code. This is also the best shelf for nurses, writers, small-business owners, and anyone tired of hearing words like "model" and "hallucination" without a plain-English explanation.

The opening resources do different jobs. Google's 45-minute introduction gives you a fast, accurate map of generative AI. Elements of AI slows down and asks what AI is for, where it shows up in society, and what it cannot do. Kaggle's ethics course turns those ideas into practical questions about fairness and privacy. Microsoft's curriculum is the deeper, self-paced option when you want to understand how the machinery works.

You do not need to complete every item. A sensible first pass is roughly 6–10 hours: Google's introduction, the first two chapters of Elements of AI, and Kaggle's ethics lessons. Add Microsoft when you want a structured 12-week curriculum, and use the People + AI Guidebook when you are designing something another person will use.

Two access notes matter. Coursera's AI for Everyone now offers a free first-module preview, not the full course and not a certificate. AWS's digital lesson is free, but its labs and credential ecosystem are separate products. The guide labels both clearly rather than calling everything "free."

**Best for:** Curious adults, career changers, healthcare workers, writers, and business owners who need useful literacy before tools or code.

## Courses, one by one

### [Introduction to Generative AI](https://www.skills.google/course_templates/536)

**Google** · Beginner · 45 min · Micro-course + quiz

A 45-minute, no-code explanation of what generative AI is, how it differs from traditional machine learning, and where Google uses it. Finish with a short quiz and a free Google skill badge.

- **Access:** Free
- **Certificate:** Free Google skill badge after passing the quiz; a Google Skills account is required.
- **Topics:** Generative AI, Foundations, No-code

### [Elements of AI: Introduction to AI](https://www.elementsofai.com/)

**University of Helsinki** · Beginner · ~30 hours · Reading + exercises

A gentle, non-technical course from the University of Helsinki and MinnaLearn. Learn what AI can and cannot do, where it shows up in daily life, and how it affects society. No math or coding required.

- **Access:** Free course
- **Certificate:** The course is free; an optional certificate costs €50.
- **Topics:** AI literacy, Society, No-code

### [Kaggle: Intro to AI Ethics](https://www.kaggle.com/learn/intro-to-ai-ethics)

**Kaggle Learn** · Beginner · 4 hours · Interactive readings + exercises

Four hours on human-centered design, fairness, and privacy for AI systems. The examples are practical, the language is plain, and you leave with a free certificate plus a better checklist for building responsibly.

- **Access:** Free
- **Certificate:** Free Kaggle Learn completion certificate.
- **Topics:** AI ethics, Fairness, Privacy

### [AI for Beginners](https://github.com/microsoft/ai-for-beginners)

**Microsoft** · Beginner · 12 weeks (self-paced) · Open curriculum + labs

Microsoft's open 12-week, 24-lesson curriculum covers AI history, neural networks, computer vision, natural language processing, and ethics, with quizzes and labs. Take only the lessons you need.

- **Access:** Free
- **Certificate:** No certificate is offered for this open curriculum.
- **Topics:** Foundations, Python, Ethics

### [AI for Everyone](https://www.coursera.org/learn/ai-for-everyone)

**DeepLearning.AI** · Beginner · ~7 hours · Coursera course

Andrew Ng's non-technical course on what AI can do, how to spot useful opportunities, and how AI projects get built. Coursera now offers a free preview of the first module; full access and a certificate are paid.

- **Access:** Free first module
- **Certificate:** Coursera preview is free and does not include a certificate. Full course and certificate require payment or approved financial aid.
- **Topics:** AI literacy, Business, No-code

### [Fundamentals of Generative AI](https://skillbuilder.aws/learn/FKXM21R555/fundamentals-of-generative-ai/ZFX96NREH4)

**AWS** · Beginner · 3 hours · Digital course

A three-hour AWS Skill Builder course on generative AI, natural language processing, and choosing an AI/ML approach for a real use case. The digital course is free; hands-on labs may require a paid subscription.

- **Access:** Free course
- **Certificate:** The digital course is free; AWS offers free digital badges for selected Skill Builder learning. Check the current course page and badge catalogue.
- **Topics:** Generative AI, Cloud, AWS

## Certificate reality

The Google Introduction to Generative AI skill badge and all listed Kaggle Learn certificates are free. Elements of AI's course is free but its optional certificate costs €50. Coursera and AWS do not hand out a free professional credential with these beginner courses. Microsoft's open curricula do not include a certificate.

A completion certificate is a record that you finished a defined piece of learning. It is not an accredited degree, a professional license, or proof that you can do the work unsupervised. See [the certificate guide](certificates.md) for the provider-by-provider rules.

## A small proof of work

Write a one-page AI field note: explain generative AI in five short paragraphs, include one prompt you tested, and list five personal rules for privacy, verification, disclosure, and when not to use AI at all.
